E.   Superintendent's Report

Academics - Presentation by Charlotte Tillman, Chief Academic Officer

Act 422: 3rd Grade Literacy Proficiency

What is Act 422?

* Mandates that students in traditional LA public schools must meet specific literacy proficiency standards to be promoted to the 4th grade.

How will students be assessed, and what do they need to score to be promoted?

*Testing: DIBELS 8th Literacy Screener; 3 opportunities to test: April, May, Summer

*3rd Grade Promotion: Scoring above the Well Below composite score of 424 and higher.
